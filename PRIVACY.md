# Privacy Policy — AI Bouncer

_Last updated: 2026-10-19_

AI Bouncer analyses text **entirely inside your browser**.

- **No data collection.** The extension does not collect, store, log or transmit any personal data, page content, pasted text, browsing history or analysis results.
- **No network requests.** All detection runs locally in the extension popup. There is no server, API, analytics or tracking.
- **No accounts, no cookies, no remote code.**
- **Page access.** When you click the extension icon, it reads the text of the *current tab only* (via `activeTab`) to analyse it locally. The text is discarded when the popup closes.
- **No sale or sharing** of data with any third party, because no data is collected.
